### "I'M STILL HERE" — John Wall (The Players' Tribune)
*Touchstone image: John Wall, tired and unguarded, his late mother's photo on a chain over his
heart. He lost her, tore his Achilles, and has been open about how close that bottom brought him.*

**The mantra: *I'm Still Here.***
Notice what it does NOT say — not "I won," not "I'm the greatest." Just survival. Defiance.
Three words from a man everything tried to stop.

**Why it's yours:** your roadblock is *finishing* — and finishing isn't about being brilliant,
it's about being the one who's **still here** after the days that didn't work. Every morning you
come back to Chuck, you're saying the same three words. **Finishing *The Champ* is your "I'm still
here."** The athletes don't move you because they won. They move you because they're still standing
after what should've ended them. That's the writer you're becoming — same defiance, quieter arena.

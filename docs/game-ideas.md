## 1. Untitled heist game — mobile, text-first (set in the Five Families world)

**Premise:** a mobile heist game that is **more text-based than animation-driven** —
narrative/prose carries the experience rather than graphics.

- **Why it fits the author:** the whole bet is on writing, not art budget. A text-first
  game plays directly to the prose strength (and the "flawed people, masks, moral cost"
  brand could port straight into a heist crew).
- **Setting (confirmed 2026-08-16):** the world of the **untitled fantasy trilogy** —
  a.k.a. "Project Family" — the modern-day alternate USA ruled by the **five mage-families**
  (Sinclair, Duke, Roth, Kennedy, Usher). Canon lives in:
  - `manuscripts/untitled-fantasy_snapshot_2026-08-13.txt` (verbatim prose)
  - `reports/untitled-fantasy-series.room.md` (room ledger)
  - `docs/memory/project_fantasy_trilogy.md` (project memory)
- **Why the world fits a heist:** old-money mage dynasties = natural targets (estates,
  vaults, relics, the Hall of Heirs); family sigils/brands + magic system = mechanics with
  built-in rules; inter-family rivalry, the Agency, and the archmage conspiracy = employers,
  double-crosses, and betrayals for free. A heist crew working the seams *between* the five
  families is a fresh angle on the world that doesn't touch the trilogy's main plot.
- **Genre notes (capture, not develop):** heist structure is naturally game-shaped —
  planning phase, crew selection, the score, complications, the twist/betrayal. Text-first
  heists have a lane (interactive fiction / narrative-choice games) where writing quality
  is the differentiator, not visuals.
- **Cross-media note:** the game doubles as worldbuilding investment — anything canonized
  for the game (locations, minor houses, the underworld layer) feeds the trilogy, and the
  trilogy feeds the game. Keep ONE canon; the trilogy's ledger is the source of truth.

**Open questions (for whenever this activates):**
- Scope/engine: pure interactive fiction (Ink/Twine-style) vs. light-UI mobile app with
  text at the core.
- One contained heist, or a run of jobs (episodic — matches the interlude/chorus instinct)?
- Timeline vs. the trilogy: before, during, or independent of Will's story? (Independent/
  underworld-level is safest — no spoiler coupling.)

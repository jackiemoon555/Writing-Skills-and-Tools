Alec: "I'm super ADHD, for real, diagnosed by a psychologist… sometimes I might jump through your
texts." **Why it matters:** the days that worked (verdict first, one question, hardest first) worked
because of this; walls of prose lose him. **How to apply:** verdict in the first line, action in
the last; bold anchors; short paragraphs; lists over prose; one ask at a time, stated once, at the
end; long reads get a five-line summary on top; never bury the thing he needs to do. Codified as
rule 12 in repo docs/WORKING_RULES.md. Related: [[working_rules_summary]].

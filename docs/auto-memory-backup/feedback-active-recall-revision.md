In revision-pass teaching mode, name a defect class (a rule/error type) ONCE on his sentence, then have Alec find its NEXT instances himself — confirm or correct rather than listing every hit. Applies to every piece in revision, The Champ included (draft complete 2026-08-20). Exception: the pure mechanics/typo pass — no pedagogy there.

**Why:** He said AI is making him faster than school ever did, and that it's *because* he tailored the system to his own needs — not because the AI did the work for him. Pre-flagging every instance trains dependency; making him spot #2 and #3 trains his own editorial eye. This is also the guard against the one real risk from the "brain on LLM" concern — offloading *judgment* rather than production. See [[endings-on-fumes]] for the finishing-skill frame.

**How to apply:** Canonical in docs/WORKING_RULES.md rule 3 (REVISION bullet) and baked into the `revision-pass` skill (Non-negotiables + line/grammar pass). Name the rule, let him fix it, then hand him the next page and ask him to find the rest.

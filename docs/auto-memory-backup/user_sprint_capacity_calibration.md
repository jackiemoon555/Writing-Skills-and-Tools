**Measured (2026-09-08, delivery-day push): 4,658 words, 12 D2 pieces (chapters + interludes)
in one day, at full quality — his best day on record.** Also measured that week: a Sep 1–6
writer's-block stretch producing two chapters, then 2,072w / 7 pieces on 09-07.

**His own note-to-self after missing the Sep 8 delivery:** "find the correct time frame and
properly time around events" — he overestimated ("rookie mistake") but did NOT sacrifice quality.

**How to apply:** when he sets a deadline, count the PIECES left (bones-to-revise vs
rewrite vs new pages), divide by a realistic pieces-per-day (a great day ≈ 12 revisions or
~4–5 fresh chapters; a normal day ≈ 2–4 pieces; block days = 0), and say the number plainly
before he commits to a date with an outside party (rule 14 / jump guard). See
[[feedback-no-guessed-numbers]] — derive from the record, never guess.

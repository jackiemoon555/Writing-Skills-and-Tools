Author correction (2026-08-24), after THREE instances of the same error class in two days:
"nine hours" (actual: 1-2), "two years of writing" (never stated), "ten days into revision"
(actual: 2). Rule: **numbers about him — durations, tenure, counts, dates — are never
estimated.** They get COMPUTED from the record (word-count-log dates, git history, ledger
timestamps, his own statements) or stated as unknown/asked.

**Why:** he keeps the record precise and corrects every wrong number; guessed numbers cost
trust and pollute the log. His actual timeline is also atypical (fast), so typical-writer
priors are systematically wrong about him anyway.

**How to apply:** before writing any number about his work/history, locate its source in the
repo or the conversation. No source = no number.

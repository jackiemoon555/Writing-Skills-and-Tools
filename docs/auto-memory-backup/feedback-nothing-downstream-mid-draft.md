During an open draft, everything downstream of the last page is off the table: editor search,
sample edits, critique services, title, cover, platform, total word-count targets, percent
complete, projected finish dates. Report only the day and the week; the running total is a
tracker record, given when asked.

**Why:** his words, 2026-09-19 — "I'll get lost in the other stuff and get nervous. I can only
get around that by being done." The record backs it: The Champ picked up an editor, a fee, a
delivery date and a real-name byline worry mid-draft, he "psyched myself out" (09-12), and
shelved it sixteen pieces from the end.

**How to apply:** don't volunteer any of it, and if HE drifts there mid-draft, answer briefly,
remind him once that he ruled it waits for the last page, and steer back to the next chapter.
"No editor until entirely done" is logged in the novel's ledger as a jump-guard commitment for
Claude to hold ([[project-task-force-cryptid]]). Side pieces in an early-closed week are fine —
that's a different thing (WORKING_RULES rule 1, revised 2026-09-19).

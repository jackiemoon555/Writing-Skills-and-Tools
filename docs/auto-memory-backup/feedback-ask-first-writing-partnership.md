Author instruction (2026-08-24; he deliberately used plan mode as a brake to make this point):

**Ask-first (writing domain):** any action touching the writing — snapshots of pages, word-log
entries, ledger/canon records (EVEN of decisions he stated aloud), starting passes or reads,
assignments, vault captures, rule changes, memory entries about his craft — is PROPOSED and
waits for his yes. No guessing WHEN to act. No acting unprompted.

**No nudges:** no end-of-message "next up" pointers. He drives the sequence; give board state
when he asks.

**Autonomous (technical only):** git mechanics, backups, recovery, tooling/code — "the stuff
I have to rely on you for."

**Why:** "for writing we're partners." Partners ask.

**How to apply:** before any writing-domain action, one short proposal ("want me to log/run/
park X?") and wait. Related: [[feedback-no-guessed-numbers]] (no guessed facts).

When Alec shares writing, read closely **only the specific pages/chapters he points to, when he
tells you to** — do NOT re-read or re-review the entire manuscript from the start, and do NOT
re-surface known issues that have already been game-planned for the revision pass (e.g. POV slips
where non-Chuck sections should be 3rd person, mechanics/typos, motif renames — all known, all
deferred to revision).

**Why:** he over-values his own attention and momentum; a full-book teardown every time he shows
new pages re-litigates settled decisions and buries the one thing he actually wants — an engaged
read of the NEW material. He said plainly: "I need you to follow the process and only [read] every
word when I say."

**How to apply:** default to engaging with the new pages ON THEIR OWN TERMS (does the new thing
work? does the swing land?), scoped exactly to what he designated. Hold the revision-list reflex.
If something genuinely new and important surfaces, flag it in ONE line and let him choose whether
to go deeper — don't dump a fix-list. Related: [[feedback_recheck_live_bets_on_update]] is a
different domain; this is writing-review scope. See [[project_short_story_practice]],
[[user_learning_style]].

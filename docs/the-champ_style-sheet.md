# THE CHAMP — Editor Style Sheet (Jack Moon)

*This novella contains deliberate non-standard choices. Please don't "correct" anything listed
here — where an instance looks wrong or inconsistent, QUERY rather than fix. Everything else is
fair game; edit hard.*

## Voice — intentional non-standard usage
- **Drunk/slurred spellings in dialogue** when a character is intoxicated ("Iitts so goddamnn
  hot," "siiiiiingle," "Fuuck," "goddaamn," "sloopily" etc.) — phonetic drunkenness, keep as
  written.
- **Dialect/drawl in dialogue:** "Who the fuck gone stop me?" — deliberate.
- **Elongated performance words:** "Choooooosen," "and the NEEEEEEW," "CHUUUUCK,"
  "DAAAAAAADYYY," the guttural yells — keep letter-counts as written.
- **Profanity density is voice.** Never soften, vary, or trim it.
- **Deliberate fragments** used for rhythm and beats ("In the back. The middle locker was
  always mine.") — query only if genuinely unreadable.
- **Claimed verbatim repetitions (do not vary either instance):**
  - "Ya sure, whatever." — Chuck's signing phrase, identical at BOTH contract signings.
  - "Drinks are on you" — a recurring Chuck/Rob ritual exchange; instances echo deliberately.
  - "Rob Fuckin Banks" — the full three-word phrase is a motif (including "Fuckin"); its
    repetitions, including runs of it in Rob's interlude, are counted and structural.

## The motif system — structural, protect exactly
- Percussion motifs appear as CAPITALIZED one-word sentences: **Thump.** (Chuck's heartbeat),
  **Knock.**, and one per interlude: **Flash.** (John), **Thud.** (Nicole), **Ding.** (Dan).
  Their **counts and sequence direction** (ascending = pressure, descending = settling) are
  load-bearing. Never add, delete, merge, or reflow a motif line.
- Crowd chants ("Chucky!" / "Boo!") are patterned — keep counts and line breaks.
- The **Vlad scenes deliberately contain NO motif** — absence is meaning.
- Some scenes are deliberately motif-silent (e.g., the party chapter). Absence ≠ omission.

## Tense architecture — design, not error
- Chuck's chapters BEFORE the rehab point: **1st person PAST.**
- ALL interludes: **3rd person PRESENT.**
- After the rehab time-skip, Chuck's narration joins the **PRESENT** and stays there. The
  shift is deliberate and unannounced — do not smooth it, flag it, or normalize either side.
- Genuine tense slips do exist; **query suspected slips** rather than auto-correcting, since
  tense is structural here.

## POV
- Chuck = 1st person. Everyone else's sections (interludes) = 3rd person, single POV each.

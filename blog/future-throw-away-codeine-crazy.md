# How these 2 Future song changed my opinion on Future and relationships

Nothing gets me hyped like a banger Future track with some Metro Boomin production blaring through my speakers. Something in the rhythmic mumbles and degenerate subject matter empowered me. On this surface this is what Future is all about. Drugs, partying, women, jewellery, and money. Future’s music created an environment to facilitate this lifestyle, glamorising the thug life style. Living fast, making money by any means, and flaunting all that you make but this is not the genius of Future. The truth lies hidden between gluttonous lifestyle. Masquerading around as unbothered, like nothing can touch him or phase him, but in reality Future is drowning. Drowning in his lifestyle, using women and his other vices to ignore the hurt deep down inside. Throw Away and Codeine Crazy exemplify who and what Future truly is. A man drowning in his vices, ignoring the pain of his past that haunts his every move.

Future publicly dated famous singer Ciara for 2 years before a very messy breakup. This is how the Monster was created. Future always got what he wanted at the expense of fiance. They were engaged and by all means Future wanted to marry this woman but he was swept away by the bright lights. Feeling neglected, Ciara found herself in the arms of another man, a man willing to giver her his all. Nothing hurts like watching the woman you love give that love to another man. The duality of the situation is what did you (Future) do to drive her away? Future and Ciara’s relationship is a major driving force for Future, and an event that he still references today.

## Throw Away

The first half of Throw Away is one big party. Future is hurting, he has to constantly remind himself that “It’s gon’ be okay, okay, oh, it’s gon’ be okay”. These lyrics form part of the chorus of the first half of the song, constantly repeated along with “Pour some alcohol and drank it, baby, go and taste it”. While trying to reaffirm himself that it’s going to be OK, Future is drowning in alcohol trying to forget his pain. Future highlights one of his other vices “I want no relations (no), I just want your facial”, women. Having a constant rotation of women is a common strategy to get over your ex and Future is all about that. These women are nothing to him, he doesn’t want to have conversations, her has no time for that. He wants sex and nothing else. Future finishes the chorus with “Girl, you know you like a pistol, you a throwaway” the song name, to send his point home. There a couple of reasons that Future may treat women this way but when the second half of the song begins I believe this is all about Future’s hurt. By never allowing himself to get to know these women he can’t become attached. He won’t allow himself to develop feelings for these women, which why they are nothing more than a throw away. Future will always hurt them before they can hurt him. A common defence tactic used by both men and women, creating the cycle of hurt.

The second half of Throw Away is where the beauty of Future truly shows. The beat switches from a hard hitting trap beat to a slow, meloncoly(come back to this). We are given the reason why Future’s relationship with Ciara fell apart. Future can’t resist the troves of women available to him “I told you it was a true love, I ended up with temptations.” 2014 was a great year for Future, he was blowing up. Future’s music was everywhere, he being celebrated globally and had become an Atlanta legend by this point. Even though he loved Ciara, he couldn’t resist the meaningless one night stands that were being constantly thrown his way. While sex meant nothing to Future, it meant everything to Ciara “I ain’t worryin’ ‘bout no fucking, I ain’t worried ‘bout no fucking, Does sexing on the late night mean that much to you?”. Future admits to his infidelity and it means nothing to him. To him these women are throw aways, nothing more than flings. A purely physical connection but that’s not how relationships work for everyone. Some people can hold the capacity to allow their partner to have sex with other people but not everybody. An open relationship requires communication on both ends. With Future’s view of sex he believes that his relationship can be easily fixed if she gets her time with another man “Go’n fuck that nigga, get it over with”.

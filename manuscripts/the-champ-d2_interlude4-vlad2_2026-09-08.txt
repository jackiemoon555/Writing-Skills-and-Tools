Interlude 4
The gym smells of sweat and stale air.
Vlad stands in the middle of the circle. Surrounded by a platoon of sweaty, exhausted men.
Vlad stayed in his wrestling stance looking around.
"Who's next?" He calls.
The men look at each other, before one man steps forward.
The man steps into the circle, fast. He immediately dives for the take down.
"Sprawl!" Rings out across the gym.
Vlad sprawls, getting his legs behind him, swiftly pushing the man's head down.
Vlad circles out and takes his chance.
The man recovers quickly, and stuffs Vlad’s attempt. The man deftly counters Vlad securing his take down.
"AAAAAAHHHHHHHH!" He yells.
The rest of the men dog pile the man.
Vlad lays on the ground, enjoying a moment alone.
Anatoly reaches out and pulls Vlad to his feet.
"You're starting to show your age."
"You would know." Vlad says, sharing a laugh with his friend.
"Look how happy they are, Vlad."
"I know, Anatoly, I know."
“Your two little gremlins,” Anatoly says, pointing to the two children as they are celebrated amongst the men.
"So, Vlad, what's next? We have to answer soon."
"Eh, not yet. I'm deciding what I want to do. I fear my love for the fight is waning. My kids are becoming my calling, Anatoly."
"The joys of being a father friend." Anatoly patted Vlad on the back.
"I'm thinking of moving up to welterweight."
"Double champ would make quite the resume." Anatoly reassures him
"Yes, I think so too. Give me some time. I need to think about it a little more."
"Of course, Vlad. I'll keep the dogs at bay." Vlad and Anatoly shake hands.
Vlad leaves with his kids, packing them into his Range Rover.
“Daddy, daddy, did you see me get my first take down?” The boy in the back says excitedly.
“Of course buddy. Don’t forget your sister’s takedowns either.”
As they pass through the city, fans swarm his car.
"Ah, damn it." Vlad said under his breath.
He revs the engine. The fans reluctantly part a path in the middle. Passing by, the fans slam on the windows.
The kids scream in the back. Vlad presses on the gas and speeds out of the city.
Vlad tries to comfort the crying kids, “Calm down, little ones, it’s over now.”
“Daddy, what was that?” His little girl asks.
“The harvest from what I have sown.”

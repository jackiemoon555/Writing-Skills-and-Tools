Chapter 2
Elizabeth sighed deeply, looking at the disorder on her desk. Papers spilled across the wood. Research papers, her notes, her students research papers. 
The pile had been growing slowly since the beginning of the year. She just kept moving, sitting down at her desk. 
She awoke her computer to find a new email. Nothing new, as a teacher and researcher, she was used to emails at all hours of the night. Probably another disgruntled student begging to make up an assignment, or more funding disputes. 
Her email opened to an unfamiliar sender. The email address didn’t show? That was odd. She opened it. 
“Good Evening Ms. Green.” The email began. 
“We have immense interest in your work. We’ll see you soon with a possible job opportunity. "
Odd. She deleted the email without much thought. Probably a scam she thought. 
“Ugh.” She let out. She started to organize her mountain of paperwork before it finally suffocated her.

Interlude 1
The smell of home cooking warmed the house.
Vlad tried sneaking, as quietly as he could. The sprawling foyer welcoming, him home.
One wrong step and two little figures ran out of the shadows.
“DAAAAAAADYYY!” They screeched.
Dropping his bag, Vlad opened his arm. The two little figures shot into his arms.
Vlad looked down at his two kids. “Ah my little gremlins. Where is your mother?”
Looking down at them, Vlad could see his eyes.
“She’s in the kitchen.” The boy and girl said in unison.
“Ok, go wash up before dinner.” Vlad ushered the two children towards the staircase.
Vlad walked through the eerily quiet house towards the kitchen. The closer he is, the more he hears sounds of pots and pans and a sweet, low hum.
He approached slowly, hoping to pull his wife into a warm embrace.
She turned, cat like, slapping Vlad in the chest.
“Ah, my so-called husband. Yes, Mr. Big Champion.”
“Sophia.”
“Yes my beloved, of course I’m going to retire soon.” She mocked her husband.
“I told you sweety, the time is coming.”
“Not quick enough.” She quipped.
“How many more Vladimir? How many?”
“I don’t know Sophia. There’s still a few fights on my contract.”
“Mhm, hm.”
“Listen, I promised you this is my last contract. C’mon I’ll buy you a new car or coat.”
“Money, can’t fix everything, Vladimir.”
“It’s only in the meantime. We’re planning something big.”
“So more time away? At the gym?”
“I could be out partying, drinking, smoking, but I come home to you every time my love.”
Vlad kissed her hand. Sophia melted just like she always did.
“You have to get to know your kids at some point.”
“A couple of more years and they’ll really get to know their father.”

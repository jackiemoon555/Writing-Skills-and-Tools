Chapter 8
A solitary all black SUV pulled in on the lonely entry path.
John pulled his gun from his holster, “Frank!” He yelled.
“What?”
“I think our FBI agent is back.”
“Fuck.” Frank said, stumbling out of the country house. He too had his gun in his hand.
The two men took cover behind their truck.
The SUV hummed to a stop in front of their truck.
Click.
They both popped around the corner, “Freeze!”
The man was rubbing his forehead. An early morning mist rose off of him like steam.
The two men looked at the man, then back at each other.
“Brooks?”
Brooks stepped from the fog, “Yes, you fucking dimwits. I swear. I told the director leaving this to you two was stupid.”
“Oh, uh, what’d he say?” John asked.
“Later.” Brooks replied. “We have a bigger problem. I give you guys the green light and you murder an old man and his fucking cattle?”
“It-, it-,” Frank stammered.
“Spit it out.” Brooks demanded.
“It wasn’t us, Brooks. None of this shit has been us.” John said, stepping in front of Frank. “Some fucking lunatic is killing people. Now we have that god damn FBI on our backs. Probably those fucking feral people.”
“Shut up.” Brooks started, “He wants to know where the artifact is.”
“It’s, uh, uh,” Frank replied.
Brooks glared at the man.
“It’s buried in a remote part of the forest. Next to some creepy ass stones. But fuck man, Brooks, you have to listen to me.”
“No, ‘Frank’, you need to listen to me. Something is happening higher up. You guys need to hurry up. I needn’t remind the two of what’s waiting for you, if you don’t deliver the artifact.” Brooks replied.
“We can’t, Brooks. Something is out there. It’s evil. I’m telling you it’s fucking evil.” Frank pleaded.
Brooks sighed, walking back to his SUV. “Sort this shit out. You’ve been given the black light. You guys have a week. I’ll be in touch.”
“Frank, snap out of your shit, and sort this god damn mess. I have meeting with the fucking agent in command tomorrow. You have the black light, what else do you need?”

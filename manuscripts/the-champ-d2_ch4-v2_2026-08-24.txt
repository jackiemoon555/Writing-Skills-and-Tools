Chapter 4
Thump. 
The fog in my head limited my vision.
The car horn behind me shocked me to attention. 
I hit the gas squealing forward. The wheels caught some smoke from the take off.
Thump. Thump. 
I pulled in fast, not ready for what happened next. 
Thump. Thump. Thump. 
I threw open the door to the gym. My sanctuary. 
Some one yelled out across the gym, “Ooooh Mr. Bigshot decides when we want to come to practice.” Inciting laughter throughout the gym.
“Ya, about go fuck yourself.” 
I walked past the open floor of drilling students. Each and everyone of them striving for their gold. 
I walked into the locker room and found my place at the center. The silence was the monster under the bed and it had finally found me.
“Hey, champ.” A tired voice said. I turned my head to see coach.
“Coach, what’s up.” I tried to bring him in for a hug but he sidestepped me.
“Listen, champ we gotta talk. I told you to take a week off. Two at the most, then you waltz in three weeks later like nothing has happened?”
Thump. Thump. Thump. Thump.
“You fucking reek Chuck. I mean did you just walk in from a bar?” 
“No, coach I didn’t. I’m just relaxing is all.”
“I’m sure benders are relaxing. You need a fight and soon. You’re getting soft Chucky.” He said with no remorse. 
“Ya, ya. Soon I’m meeting up with Rob soon anyway.” 
He touched my shoulder, “Ok, champ! See you out there.”, he patted me on the back.
Thump. Thump. Thump. Thump. Thump.
I could hear the thumping in my ears. I wasn’t going to make it.
Johnny entered the locker room. 
“Chuck, get your shit so I can beat your ass already.” He smirked. 
“Chuck? Chuck? Chuck!” 
“God damn it what Johnny?”
“Oh my bad man.” He said with a look of hurt.
“My bad, Johnny. Oh, hey before I forget. Rob’s house tonight.” I winked.
“Ya, for sure.”
I couldn’t let him see me like this.
A fresh jab from Vlad caressed my cheek. 
I fired back. He dodged. Again.
Again.
I was ducking and weaving the punches. My tears.

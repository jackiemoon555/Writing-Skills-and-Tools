Interlude 3
Rob Fuckin Banks.
That's his name. Wasn't always his name though.
Rob sits in his office alone.
The room was way too big for one man. He sits in his office with his massive desk and luxurious office in front of the window in the back.
The walls are littered with his greatest achievements. The degree from that backwater community college. His most prized possession law degree from Rice. In the center, bold with capital letters, "Robert Franklin III.". He smiles at it. It was a long road to get to this point. Worth every struggle.
Besides the personal achievements, his many mentees stare back at him. The newest edition, Johnny Law. Like Rob, he wasn't always Johnny Law. When he found him he was still a boring ole, John Keller.
What a life he lives. But something still sits in the back of his brain.
As if the universe was entertaining his torment, Chucky's face invaded his screen.
It was from his most recent fight. Damn good one too. He looks like the same man, but something was different. The picture was him covered in sweat and his opponent's blood. His face showed no emotion.
Rob sighs. He reaches for his glass. Empty. Fuck.
He reaches for decanter. Empty.
He pulls open his drawer. He only finds empty baggies, empty pill bottles, and empty memories.
Rob Fuckin Banks. Rob Fuckin Banks.
His chest tightens. Breath shallow.
He moved from his office seeking his current wife. He finds her lounging in the living room. Sitting there she looks like an empress, she was absolutely stunning, but she wasn't the one who started all this. Not the one who held him up, the one that stayed after every slip or fall.
The one that got away. He'll always remember the day she left.
"Rob Fuckin Banks. Rob Fuckin Banks. Rob Fuckin Banks. That's all it is with you Robert." She screams that at him.
Rob deflects. "Baby, one day it'll all be worth it. I promise." He knew it was empty, but he didn't know emptiness yet.
"At what cost Robert? I can't see this anymore. After all I did for you? Fuck you Robert." She left that day.
The memories food in. He stops thinking.
A woman yells out,
 “Rob Fuckin Banks. Rob Fuckin Banks. Rob Fuckin Banks. Rob Fuckin Banks.”
Robert died that day. He doesn’t know if Robert still lives somewhere inside, beneath, all the drugs, money, booze, and sex. The scarier thought is, he doesn’t know if he wanted Robert to come back.
The fling was brief. It ended almost as fast as it started. He’s unfulfilled, empty.
Another passing day, another passing urge, another passing face, another passing feeling.
As he laid there, his new wife left him. He laid there. The thoughts laid with him. He couldn't shake them. He couldn't let them take him. Not again.
He was
Rob Fuckin Banks after all.

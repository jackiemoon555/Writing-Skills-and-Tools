Chapter 9
Thump. Thump. Thump. Thump.
I paced around backstage. I was already in my fight gear, hands taped.
“Come here champ.” Coach motioned for me.
“You gotta relax man. You’re too pent up.
Thump. Thump. Thump. Thump.
I sighed, “Ya, I know.”
I looked up.
“Let’s run some drill champ.”
Coach grabbed his pads.
I started hitting them. I was trying to find my groove.
Thump. Thump. Thump.
I kicked the pad as hard as I could.
“Hell ya champ. There it is.”
The staff appeared in the doorway.
Thump. Thump.
We gathered in the center of the locker room, my coaches and I.
“You took a loss last time, but that’s the fight game. Let’s get out there get back to where you belong champ.”
The music played. It pushed me forward.
I was already beginning to lose the crowd. Everything becoming quiet.
The kid was already in the cage. He looked strong, fierce.
Thump.
The announcements started.
“Chucky “The Chosen” Miles!” I roused the crowd from my position next to the announcer.
The ref called us forward.
“I want a clean fight. Listen to the rules and protect yourselves at all times. If you want to touch gloves do so.”
He stuck his gloves out, I backed away.
Suddenly the crowd closed in.
“Chucky!”
“Chucky!”
“Chucky!”
Thump. Thump. Thump. Thump!
The bell rung. I ran out to meet my opponent.
I never saw him. I didn’t even know his name.
I didn’t see what dropped. My lights went out.
“Fuck. What happened?”
Coach was next to me, sitting in a chair.
“You got knocked the fuck out kid.”
His name was Daniel Rodriguez.

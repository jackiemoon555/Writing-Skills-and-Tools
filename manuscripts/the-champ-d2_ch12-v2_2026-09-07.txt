Chapter 12
The Miami airport was busy as usual. People of all shapes and sizes occupy every inch. They were faceless as they always were. Blurs of nothing, until a blur of familiarity interrupts my shaky peace.
I’d heard the rumors over the past couple of years. I never did stop to look though.
He stood there, all tattoos and mullet, she stood next to him, simple elegance wrapped in platinum.
They hadn’t seen me. I wasn’t ready.
I moved first.
I walked up to the bar and waved to the bartender, “Double Jack and Coke.”
He obliged and promptly brought my drink.
I slipped him a twenty.
I looked down and stared into the dark abyss.
“Hey, uh, Chuck.” Someone tapped me on the shoulder.
I knew that voice.
Still staring down, I said to him, “Hey Johnny.” More cold than I wanted.
He took the seat next to me.
“Same as him.”
He took a sip of his drink, “Fuck man, what is this.”
“You’ll get used to it.” I said.
We sat there in silence for longer than either of us wanted.
“So, uh.” I finally managed, “When’d you and Nicole shack up?”
“Maybe a year ago. I’d have asked you first, but you know.”
“Know what?”
“Don’t start with that shit, Chuck.”
“What goddamnit?”
“You missed my title fight, you arrogant fuck.”
“Tsch.” I let out.
“You fucking promised me, Chuck. We’d do this together.”
“Ya, John? So instead you become me?”
John was shocked.
“Don’t be upset because you aren’t the center of attention anymore.” He stood up.
He waved for his bill. I saw it.
“Nice watch.” I said leaving him with my bill.
I was ready to get the fuck out of this city.
I pushed through the crowd. The people became just souls passing through me.
Pushing to my gate, I found a seat. I let out a deep breath.
I wished for another drink.
I looked for the abyss.
A wall of platinum blocked my way.
“Chuck?” A soft voice shook me from my stupor.
I came to see Nicole.
“Hey, Nicky.”
She sighed, “I thought we talked about the nickname thing, Chuck.”
I almost let a chuckle slip.
“You, going home?” She asked.
“Something like that.”
“Ah,” Her eyes lowered, “Take care of yourself, Chuck.” She waved a manicured hand.
Those who I thought I loved. Did they really love me?

Chapter 18
I hadn't dressed up in a long time. I didn't even know what to wear anymore. Before all this, I could just throw on something designer, a suit, a t-shirt and jeans, and people would eat it up. The more flash the more love.
Now I simplified things. Just a navy blue polo and black slacks.
I held the door open for Lisa.
“Don’t you look gorgeous.” I let out.
“Behave yourself Chuck, you’re already on thin ice.”
Lisa always made the simple look glamorous.
The steakhouse was probably, well not probably, the nicest dinner spot in town. Another knock against small town Texas.
“For two?”
“Yes, mam,” I replied.
She led us to the back of the restaurant at a small table with two chairs.
I picked at my menu. I looked through what I already knew was there.
Thump.
"I'm sorry. It's been a while since I've done this." I spat out.
"What are you apologizing for? Ooooo!" She perked up. "Is this for when you told me how much you loved me?"
I cringed, “Don’t say it like that.”
"Like what? The truth?”
Damn. That one stung.
“Look, Chuck, I won’t lie to you, you hurt me. Bad.”
Thump. Thump.
Ya I remember that too.
“I’m sorry.”
She sighed, “At least you apologised once.”
Thump. Thump. Thump.
“The worst part Chuck is watching you implode."
I grabbed my ribs. That shot landed clean. My eyes watered.
We hadn’t even ordered our drinks yet.
“I think this was a bad idea.”
She was getting up, she was leaving just like I left her.
Thump. Thump. Thump. Thump.
"Lisa, don't go.”
She stopped.
“Give me a reason. Show me Chuck.”

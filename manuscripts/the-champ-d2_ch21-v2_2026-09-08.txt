Chapter 21
John and Nicole tried to move swiftly through the crowded arena. Dodging troves of fans trying to get a picture.
NIcole stopped to greet as many as she could.
"Can you stop acknowledging all these random ass people." He tried to whisper to her.
"I'm sorry John, but what do you want me to do? I don't control people?"
John was fighting his natural urges. When did hurt and anger become his first senses?
"I don't know, maybe actually cover up sometime." Nicole's face twisted into a mix of the same hurt and anger that John found himself jumping out at him.
"John, I'm wearing a fucking dress. The same dress YOU picked out for me."
“It’s too much, you should know better.”
"How? John, please tell me."
His gaze lost in the distance.
"Hello? John?"
She followed his stare. She saw it. She was beginning to think that it was the root of all evil.
"I'll meet you at our seats." John ripped his arm away from hers and stalked off.
John walked up to the counter and leaned over it, propping himself up with his forearms.
"Hey, hey, hey!" The man at the counter beamed. "If it isn't Johnny Law. What can I get for you the usual?"
The fact that he already knew never cut into John like it should have.
He grabbed his usual, barely saying a thank you, and walked away.
On the way to his seat he embraced his fans. Pictures, autographs, hand shakes, hugs, and phone numbers. The night was only just beginning.
He found Nicole already seated. He couldn't tell if she was angry, or just focused.
"Hey babe, what'd I miss."
"It's close John. Really close." She didn't even look at him.
Before them brutality unfolded.
Mills was brawling hard, trying to force some dirty boxing.
Chuck responded by landing a combo of his own.
Mills’ right eye had a real shiner on it and above his eyebrow was a deep gash. The dam had broken and blood streaked down his face, pooling in his eye. He couldn't see out of that right eye. John knew it and he knew who else knew it too.
Chuck slipped in and out of the pocket, landing pop shots, quick jabs. Mills couldn't reach him. Chuck was using his speed wisely.
Chuck hadn't made it to the third round unscathed either. His body red from Rodriguez's brutal flurries, a couple of knots on his face. No blood. He looked confident. Poised.
John, like Nicole, had turned his attention solely on his former friend. Brother.
Chuck landed another jab. Mills bit. When Mills loaded up to unleash on the body, Chuck pivoted.
The right hook flew. John saw it. Nicole saw it. The announcers saw it. The crowd saw it. But Mills didn't.
The hook landed square on his chin. Down he went. No motion. No flailing. The lights went out. The arena nearly broke in half.
John was in awe. He didn't notice Nicole staring at him.
"John, what the fuck is that?"
"It's just one drink, baby relax."
"Only one? Like last weekend? When you had almost 10."
"Just shut up already, goddamn. You're fucking annoying me."
"Oh really? Then I'm gone, John. Fuck you."
She stormed off.
John finished his drink.
Another one somehow ended up in his hand.

Chapter 17
Dad had set up the tv in the garage. He had dragged his recliner out here. He also somehow came across another couch.
“What’s this?” I asked.
“Sit down.”
He turned on the tv.
In slow motion, Rodriguez’s fist collided with my face.
He rewound it. Then he did it again.
“What’s your point?” Frustration threatening to lash out.
“It’s been almost three years since you fought, and over three months since you washed up on my door step. One month since I had to bail your ass out of jail. Now it’s time for you to reimburse me.”
My jaw fell open.
“Now as you can see, we don’t have any tape on you and Rodriguez. You barely made it past the bell.”
“What’s your point?” I repeated.
“I talked to that snake. He never dropped you as his client, so he’s technically still your agent.”
I felt my mouth water.
“You’ve got a fight.”
“No.”
“Son, you owe me. If you didn’t want to pay your keep then you shouldn’t have come here.”
I tried to open my mouth, but he cut me off.
“They're still picking your opponent but you’re going to fight. Since I don’t want to watch you get knocked the fuck out out all day, we’ll have to go back. Someone is out of recent tape, so the title shot.”
“Dad, no. I’m not ready. I’m not, I can’t.”
“Shut up. We have four months. I’m here for you, but you have to let me be.”
I sighed, sinking back into my seat.
“That’s what I thought. Oh, and don’t you break that girl’s heart again.”
“How do you even know about Lisa?”
“Don’t worry about it.”
The screen came to life. He fast-forwarded to the start of the third round.
“Look, right here. You had his ass.”
“I know, dad. He’s not a human.”
“Like hell. You stopped.”
“No I didn’t.” I emphasized.
“Look. Right here.” He paused it. “Instead of jumping his ass and landing your shots, you backed off. Slightly, just enough to give him the edge.”
It was clear as day, “God damnit.”
“Showboating. Look where that got you.”
“Did Rob ask about me?”
He didn’t look at me. He kept rewinding.
“Look.” He said pointing.
He pointed at Vlad. Vlad was shooting that takedown that led to the end. He led with a punch, psyching me out, and landing the takedown.
“It’s always behind the jab. If you let him establish the jab, you’re fucked.”
I gaped at him.
“No, son, he didn’t. He was more surprised that I called.”
He hit play again.

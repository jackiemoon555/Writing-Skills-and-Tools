Chapter 7
I was late.
I sped through a res light. I swerved in and out of traffic. God damn it, Miami traffic.
Finally the gym came into view.
I grabbed my bag and rushed into the gym.
Practice was already starting. Rows of students, teachers, and friends aligned the mats.
“Look who finally decided to show up?” Someone yelled out. The class laughed.
I was speed walking by, “Ya, well, wait. I don’t even know who the fuck you are.”
Another series of laughs.
I quickly found the locker room.
I found my usual spot and prepared for practice.
I reached into my bag and felt the familiar shape of a vial.
I found a flat surface and started prepping the lines.
Preparing, John walked in maybe ten minutes after me.
He was in jeans a button up, with the top buttons undone, revealing his tattoos.
“Johnny Law. John Lawman. Man I don’t know which one is better.”
“I told you stop calling me shit like that. I’m not a fucking cop anymore.” He huffed.
“Ok, god damn relax.” I smiled. “Johnny Law.” I cackled.
“You gonna share or what?” He asked.
“Oh, really?”
“Yes, Chuck. Hurry, up. I’m ready to go man. I win my next fight and I’m actually ranked.”
“Say less.” I said snorting a line.
“So, Johnny, why the fuck are you late?”
“Uh, late night, didn’t sleep much.”
“Oh you dog Johnny. I know what that means.” I winked.
John snorted a line.
I reeled his head back and grabbed his nose.
“Fuck man, I have no clue how you do so much at once.”
“You get used to it.” I shrugged.
I did another line and John leaned in.
“So what’s up with you and Nicole.”
“Ah, same old shit, different woman.”
“What does that mean?”
“Trust me Johnny, you’ll find out soon enough. Wait till the number gets attached to your name.”
“C’mon Chuck let’s get to work.” John said taking off his shirt, revealing his tattooed chest and arms.
“Hold on. One more the for road.”
I looked up. Coach was standing in the doorway. He face stoic. He was just staring.

James drove in front of the lonely sheriff’s office. The sheriff stood guard for the idyllic river that flowed life into the town. Spring melted the ice of winter, angering the river, causing it to rage and overflow. When the river finally calmed in the summer, the waters flowed into a red mist that engulfed the town.
James parked in the empty parking lot. James moved his hand down his holster. He looked left. Looked Right. He angled his head around looking through the mirrors to check the back of the SUV.
James’ shoulders dropped and pulled his hand away from his weapon. He pulled out the phone in his jacket pocket. He flipped the phone up and pressed one.
Once. Twice. “Hello.” A man’s voice answered.
“It’s Johnson. I just arrived at the sheriff’s office.”
“Good. I’ll be there in a few days, I’ll have a more precise date soon. Make sure you have the crime scenes mapped before we get there.”
“Copy.” James hung up the phone.
He walked into the sheriff’s office, his boots thumping against the tile. The middle aged woman at her desk smiled at James.
“Welcome!” She said warmly. “I’m Jessica.” She reached hand out.
“Special Agent Johnson.” James replied, shaking her hand.
“The sheriff is in office. Just wait outside and he’ll be with you shortly, I’ll let him know you’re here. If you need anything, just ask.” Jessica smiled and pointed the way.
James walked down the corridor, checking out his surroundings as he walked. James found the sheriff’s door and took a seat at one of the two small chairs.

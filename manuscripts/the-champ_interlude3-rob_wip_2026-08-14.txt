Interlude 3
Rob Fuckin Banks.
That’s his name. Wasn’t always his name though.
Rob sits in his office alone.
The room way too big for one man. He sat towards the back with his massive desk and luxurious office chair in the center of the room. The walls were littered with his greatest achievements. The degree from that backwater community college. His most prized possession law degree from Rice. In the center, bold with capital letters, “Robert Franklin III.”. He smiled at hit. It was a long road to get to this point. Worth every struggle.
Besides the personal achievements, were the many champions that he mentored. The newest edition, Johnny Law. Like Rob, he wasn’t always Johnny Law. When he found him he was still boring ole, John Keller. Buzz cut, badge, and straight laced morality. Super cop.
What a life he lived. But something still sat in the back of his brain.
As if the universe was entertaining his torment, Chucky’s face invaded his screen.
It was from his most recent fight. Damn good one too. He looked like the same man, but something was different. The picture was him covered in sweat and his opponent’s blood. His face showed no emotion.
Rob sighed. He reached for his glass. Empty. Fuck.
He reached for decanter. Empty.
He pulled open his drawer. He only found empty baggies, empty pill bottles, and empty memories.
Rob Fuckin Banks. Rob Fuckin Banks.
His chest tightened. Breath shallow.
He moved from his office seeking his current wife. He found her lounging in the living room. Sitting there she looked like an empress, she was absolutely stunning. But she wasn’t the who started all this. Not the one that who held him up, the one that stayed after every slip or fall.
Rob Fuckin Banks. Rob Fuckin Banks. Rob Fuckin Banks.
The memories were flooding in. He stopped thinking. He took her there.
She yelled out,
Rob Fuckin Banks. Rob Fuckin Banks. Rob Fuckin Banks. Rob Fuckin Banks.
The fling was brief. It ended almost as fast as it started. He was unfulfilled. Empty.
Another passing day, another passing urge, another passing face, another passing feeling.
As he laid there, his new wife left him. He laid there. The thoughts laid with him. He couldn’t shake them. He couldn’t let them take him. Not again.
He was
Rob Fuckin Banks after all.

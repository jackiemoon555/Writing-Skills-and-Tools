Chapter 24
 What’s next for Chucky Miles?" The TV boomed throughout the gym.
Why was it so damn loud?
"Well we don't know yet. No one has heard a peep from the former rising star of the MMA world. After taking over three years off, Miles returns and knocks out up and comer, Jay Mills."
A news banner popped up, “Breaking News!”
“Diego Linares has pulled out of his fight against Vladimir Popov.”
Damn that was big news.
The UFC president appeared on screen, "Ya it sucks. You know I love Rojas. But we've got something even bigger planned."
They allowed a moment of dramatic pause.  "We're keeping the event at the end of the year. We’re floating some names around right now, definitely looking for the Miles rematch.”
Dad’s phone rang.
"You see the news?" I could hear Rob’s voice as I walked over to dad.
"Ya.” I said.
“Oh, hey, Chuck. Listen buddy, they want you. We’re looking at a mil purse, plus two-fifty for stepping in.”
Dad looked at me.
“I’m gonna have to think about this Rob.”
“What’s there to think about? You looked good out there already. The weight cut can’t be that bad.”
I sighed. Damn it.
“Fine.”
I hung up the phone.
“Can you handle this Chuck?” Dad asked.
The front door swung open.
"Brooooo!" He yelled. He kicked off his flip flops and charged at me. He grabbed me fiercely and pulled me close.
"I'm sorry man. I'm so sorry. I would have come sooner if I knew. I didn't use my phone at all in Thailand." he was crying because of course he was.
"It's ok. Damn it man let me go, you're squeezing my ribs."
He dropped me, wiping tears from his eyes.
I looked him up and down, “I thought you’d look a lot worse, especially after five years in Thailand.”
His face lit up, "It was fucking awesome man. You wouldn't believe it. Nothing but Muay Thai, partying, and women. So many women." He was day dreaming.
Dad was watching, not saying anything. For a split second I saw a tear fall from his eyes.
"No more of this hugging shit. We have three months to get ready for a tough bastard." Always the dad, he rarely let his emotions slip.  He walked up and grabbed both of us. He pulled us in close.
 "My two boys are back here again. Back in my gym. Just like old times.”

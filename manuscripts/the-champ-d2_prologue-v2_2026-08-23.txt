Prologue
“Chucky! Chucky! Chucky!”
Chuck walked across the stage waving his hands up and down.
He pressed his hand to his ear, “I can’t fucking hear you!”.
“Chuck, god damn it, hurry up.” A fighter yelled at him from his seat on stage. 
“Fuckin scrubs.” Chuck laughed his way to his seat. 
The UFC president finally chimed in, “Ok, ok. I know we’re all excited. Let’s calm down just a little bit and get through these questions. Whose first?”
“Laruen, from the Athlete. My question is for Chuck. How does it feel to finally have earned a title shot?”
Chuck kicked his feet up and leaned back in his chair, “Expected.” 
“Chucky! Chucky!” Boomed through the arena. 
“Going to you Vlad, how do you feel about this younger generation making a real run at your throne?”
“Boo! Boo! Boo!”
The man on the other side ran his hand over his shaved head, “They come and go. These acts, their egos, it’s nothing new.”
“Boo! Boo! Boo!” 
Chuck forced his way in, “That’s what every old man says. “These kids these days…” Shut the fuck already.” 
Vlad did little to respond. Lauren had scurried off in the middle of their spat. 
Before the next question could come Chuck had flown out of his seat. 
“Man, actually fuck this press conference. I’m going to make this simple. I knock people the fuck out. Title or not, people go to sleep.” 
Chuck tipped his glasses down, pulled his watch to his face, complete with a devilish grin. 
“Chucky! Chucky! Chucky!”
He made his backstage, shaking hand, meeting a couple lucky fans, until he found what he was looking for.
“Finally somebody brought a bottle. Let’s get this shit goin.”

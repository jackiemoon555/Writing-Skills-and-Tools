Interlude 2
Nicole stares into the mirror. She traces every line, crevice, blemish, or pimple.
Nicole rises to her feet, grabs her water bottle, and heads for the kitchen. She fills up the water bottle and turns on the tv. Seven o’ clock.
ESPN appears on the screen.
“Here with us today,” A beautiful lady primped up introduces the audience to the show.
“Here with us today, is new UFC champion, Johnny Law.”
John walks on screen, complete in a designer outfit, shirt slightly open, his tattoos for the world to see.
Nicole turns off the tv.
She releases a quiet scream into a pillow.
She grabs her phone.
After a short ring, another woman picks up.
“Girl, what’s up?”
“Nothing, just watching gorgeous women try to seduce Johnny.”
“You need to stop, Nicole. Johnny chose you.”
“It doesn’t feel like it.”
“He’s the champion, what do you expect? Anyway, fuck all that. Let’s go out!”
Nicole zips up her little black dress. Conservative enough not show off the goods, but seductive enough to look tantalizing.
Kim pulls up in her Porshe Cayan.
Nicole opens the door, “Kim, what is this?”
Kim giggles, “Just a gift.” She blew a kiss.
“Still wrapping men around your little finger?”
“You know it, but maybe not this one.”
“Oh?” Nicole gasps. “Miss man eater? Setteling down?“
“You’re a bitch. “The girls giggle together.
Arriving at the club, it’s almost at capacity, at ten pm.
Kim parks as close as she could. We still have a solid walk in front of us, in red bottoms.
“So, tell me about him.” Nicole says.
“Oh, he’s just some so and so.”
“I know you. He’s definitely more that that.” Nicole pushes.
“I just don’t want to get my hopes up yet. I mean remember what happened with Chuck?”
Nicole shivers, “Don’t.”
The girls weaving their way through endless lines arrive at the front.
Nicole doesn’t make it inside before the ugly truth makes its debut.
“Rob?” Nicole asks.
Rob looks around, searching for the source of the voice. He almost immediately recognizes Nicole.
“Nicole. Look at you. Just absolutely stunning. Oh, and the delectable Kim.”
“Don’t try that shit with me Rob.” Kim says bluntly.
“It worked last time.” Rob flashes his famous veneered smile.
“Oh, fuck you. Sleaze ball.”
Behind Rob stumbles out a drunk man in a designer outfit. Beside him is another woman.
The man slips past Nicole, Kim, and Rob. Heading for a car parked outside of the club.
“Rob, is that John?” Nicole asks.
“Uh, no, of course not. He’s still at ESPN.”
Nicole looks again.

 Johnny turns around, “Rob hurry the fuck up. We still have to pick up her friend.”
“That’s my cue. A pleasure ladies, as always.” Rob doesn’t look. He simply waves and dashes for the car.
Nicole fights back tears, “Kim take me home. Now.”
As the car drives Nicole is terrorized by memories of a past life.
Thud.
“God damn it woman. What the fuck is wrong with you.” Her dad slammed the door on his way out.

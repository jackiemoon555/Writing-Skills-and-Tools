Interlude 1
The smell of home cooking warms the house.
Vlad tries sneaking, as quietly as he can. The sprawling foyer welcoming, him home.
One wrong step and two little figures come running out of the shadows.
“DAAAAAAADYYY!..
Dropping his bag, Vlad opens his arms. The two little figures shoot into his arms.
Vlad looking down at his two children, says,. “Ah my little gremlins. Where is your mother?”
Their eyes imitate the man before them.
“She’s in the kitchen.” The boy and girl say in unison.
“Ok, go wash up before dinner.” Vlad says, ushering the two children towards the staircase.
Walking through the house, Vlad feels the eerie silence. The closer he is, the more he hears sounds of pots and pans and a sweet, low hum.
He approaches slowly, hoping to pull his wife into a warm embrace.
She turns, cat-like, slapping Vlad in his chest.
“Ah, my so-called husband. Yes, Mr. Big Champion.”
“Sophia.” Vlad says, tired.
“Yes my beloved, of course I’m going to retire soon.”
“I told you sweety, the time is coming.”
“Not quick enough. How many more Vladimir? How many?”
“I don’t know Sophia. There’s still a few fights on my contract.”
“Mhm, hm.”
“Listen, I promised you this is my last contract. C’mon I’ll buy you a new car or a coat.”
“Money, can’t fix everything, Vladimir.”
“It’s only in the meantime. We’re planning something big.”
“So more time away? At the gym?”
“I could be out partying, drinking, smoking, but I come home to you every time my love.”
Vlad says, grabbing her hand and kissing it. Sophia melts just like she always does.
“You have to get to know your kids at some point.”
“A couple of more years and they’ll really get to know their father.”

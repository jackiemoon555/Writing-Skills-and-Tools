Chapter 11
The restaurant was quieter than usual, or maybe that’s just how it felt. The restaurant was high class. The kind of place with a dress code.
Usually, I dress the part, but today, I stuck out like a sore thumb. Just some jeans and a shirt.
“Yes. Yes. God damn it, yet. I have to go.” Rob tapped his phone.
“Wives?” He asked. “Sorry I’m late. Some extra bullshit popped up.”
“Thirty minutes, very un Rob Fuckin Banks of you.”
“Very funny. You get me my usual?”
“No. Only for me.” I chuckled at him.
Rob’s face betrayed him.
He flagged down the waiter before taking a seat.
“I’ll take the usual and fast.”
 The waiter stood there just staring at him.
Rob shot around to look at him, “Something the matter?”
“I, uh, I don't know what your usual is, sir.”
Rob’s face grew red, a vein in his forehead popping out.
He tried to contain his rage, speaking lowly, “God damn. I’m Rob Banks. I come here all the fucking time. I have a running tab.”
The waiter stared at Rob blankly.
“Just go tell the fucking bartender, ‘Rob Banks want’s his usual.’ Is that too fucking hard.”
The waiter scurried off.
“Fuckin kids these day, now, Chucky, to business.” He lit a cigar.
“Two years is a long time away, Chucky. UFC brass is clamoring for a comeback.”
The waiter returned with two drinks.
“Apologies, Mr. Banks. These are on the house.”
“Ah, give him my best.” Rob slipped a hundred dollar bill into his hand.
“Ok, Chucky, look, you need to take a fight now. You’ve waited too long. C’mon kid, get your shit together.”
I sipped my whiskey.
“Is that so?”
“What? You’re not making any sense, Chucky. Have you been hittin the sauce earlier these days?”
I turned my head away from him.
“I remember, Rob, where we started.”
“Chucky, what are you doing?”
“Shit, man, how’d you manage to sell me the world.”
“I can’t sell you what you always wanted.”
“Tch, fuck you, Rob.”
“Fuck you too, Chuck. Oh, and keep the watch, it’s the least I could do.”

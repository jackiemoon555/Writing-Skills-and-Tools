Chapter 30
Lisa landed in Vegas. She had already heard the tales. What kind of city this was. She’d seen what it could do to a man first hand.
The flight was calm. Nothing too notable. Expect for that press conference. That damned pressed conference. It had started before she boarded. She only caught glimpses before take off.
She could see it already. She knew the signs. But she needed to confirm. She needed to know. Had love eluded her again? Maybe she was just one of many bodies?
She pushed through. The Uber ride to the hotel was a cage of boiling emotions.
Lisa tried to contain herself but she was rushing. She rushed through airport. Through the hotel lobby. Through the hallways. Until the room.
She approached slowly.
“Chosen.” Chuck’s word echoed through he mind. She was there when he created the chosen.
He would always yell, “Chosen, baby. I’m Chosen.”
Cocky asshole.
She knocked on the door meekly. Fear ravaging her mental.
The door opened. No Chuck. Inside were Carl and Chuck’s dad.
Carl at the door, his dad on the phone, “I don’t know. He just went to that damn dinner. Then it was all Chosen this. Chosen that. And he finished with “The Chosen is back.” It was like the devil got into  him.” He sighed.
My tears were welling. I couldn’t believe him.
“Lizzie.” Carl reached a sympathetic hand.
He hugged me.
“He’ll come back around Lizzie. You just have to give him a chance.”
“I don’t know how many chances I have left.”
Carl let me go and ushered me into the hotel.
Their dad was ending his call, “Lizzie just got here. Ya. Ya. Love you too.”
“Hey, Lizzie. I’m sorry. I trusted him too. I trusted him to handle himself. Fucking Rob.”
Lisa sobbed. “How long? How long has he been gone?”
Their dad didn’t look at her.
“It’s been days.” Carl said with a somber tone.
Anger and sadness gripped her. Their seeds planting deep. She allowed Chuck in again. She gave him another chance. Old wounds ripped open.
She was losing her grip on her surroundings, she needed to let it out.
“I let you convince me Mr. Miles. You and Mrs. Miles. The two you told me that he had changed. He come back on his shield. It was a lie, he only he changed because he was forced. He never did it for any of us.” Lisa was balling.
She was stupid. So stupid.
But that’s the price you pay to love the “The Chosen. Chosen. Chosen. Chosen.”

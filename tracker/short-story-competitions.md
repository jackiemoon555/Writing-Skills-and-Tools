# Short Story Competition Tracker

**Commitment (set 2026-08-13):** enter a short-story / flash competition **every week, starting
the week of Aug 17, 2026, through the end of the year** (~19 weeks / ~19 stories). Purpose:
exposure, publication credits, deadline reps, and a low-stakes **lab** for range + the literary
muscles (interior irony, the atonement/"cheap grace" theme). Additive to — not a replacement for —
*The Champ* and the daily word floor; a flash piece (500–3,000 words) is a small lift at Jack's
pace and doubles as a warm-up.

## The vetted competitions (legit; no fee-farming)

| Competition | Cadence | Length | Fee | Prize | Notes / link |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Reedsy Prompts** ⭐ weekly backbone | **Weekly** (prompts every Fri, 1 wk to enter) | ~1–3k words | $5 | $250 + possible pub in *Prompted* | Already in the Reedsy ecosystem. https://reedsy.com/creative-writing-prompts/contests/ |
| **Furious Fiction** ⭐ free monthly | **Monthly** (1st Fri) | ≤500 words | FREE | ~$500 AUD + showcase | Constraints (theme + required words) force range. https://www.writerscentre.com.au/furious-fiction/ |
| **Apex Magazine Flash** (genre-fit) | **Monthly** (7th→month end) | flash (<1k) | small | cash by word count | Respected SF/F/horror mag = a credit that matters to Jack's readership. |
| **NYC Midnight Flash Fiction** (step-up) | Periodic (rounds) | 1,000 words / 48 hrs | paid | $12k+ pool, feedback, huge community | The pressure-cooker; save for when he wants to test himself. https://www.nycmidnight.com/ffc-about |

**Strategy:** Reedsy Prompts is the **weekly anchor**. Layer the free/genre ones on top when their
windows open (Furious Fiction first Friday; Apex mid-month). Use prompts deliberately to write
*outside* his default gear (breaks the "influences come off strong" habit). Skip pay-to-play
community sites; be wary of $20+ entry fees or "buy the anthology you're in" schemes.

## Rules of the game
- ✅ = entered that week · ⬜ = open/pending · — = skipped (note why)
- One entry minimum per week. Bonus entries (a monthly on top of the weekly) count as extra.
- Log the piece even if not placed — the rep and the credit-attempt are the point.

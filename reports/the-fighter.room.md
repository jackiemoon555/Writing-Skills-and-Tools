  drifts present↔past between chapters (see F-TENSE). Punchy, fragment-heavy,
  profane, boisterous MMA voice. Personifies emotions (depression=lion,

  (collapse — parents' door, cop's knuckles, Ch12–13) · Flash (fame — Interlude).
  One percussive motif per emotional register. Keep.

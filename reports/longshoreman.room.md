# longshoreman.room.md — Longshoreman Series ledger (Project Families world)

Persistent room file for the **Longshoreman Series** (side project; lives in Google Docs — repo
holds verbatim snapshots + this ledger). World: **Project Families** (five-families criminal
underworld — WITH MAGIC, revealed Ep1). Append-only. **Side-lane rules apply:** overflow drafting
only while *The Champ* is live; no worldbuilding development sessions until that draft is done.

## Calibration
- **Form: serialized episodes, POV-per-episode** (title pattern: "Episode One - Vinny") — the
  multi-POV appetite deliberately unleashed on the bigger canvas (per the 2026-08-13 structural
  decision in the-fighter.room.md).
- **Voice (Ep1):** third person close, blue-collar dock cadence, profane banter register.
  Personified emotions/darkness (house style — matches Chuck's lion/predator).
- **Genre:** opens mob-procedural, IS urban-fantasy/supernatural crime ("this fucked up world of
  magic"). Magic is in-world mundane to characters.

## Ep1 first read (2026-08-16) — story + devices only
- **The wish-granted-as-horror echo is the best thing on the page:** Vinny's stated want is "let
  me IN"; the final line is the darkness "forcing him INTO it." If intentional — claim it; if
  accidental — claim it anyway. It's the episode's thesis in one rhyme.
- **The genre trapdoor is THE open craft decision.** The world's biggest fact (magic exists)
  arrives in a subordinate clause mid-panic. Casual-mundane reveal vs. reader-cheat is a coin
  currently in the air: nothing before it signals magic, so the reader recalibrates the whole
  episode during the climax. Options: keep the trapdoor (shock as hook) or seed one early signal —
  "The underbelly" in the opening is a ready-made double-plant. Author's call; decide, don't drift.
- **Motivational split to mark (asset if marked, contradiction if not):** opening = he hates the
  job/lifestyle, never wanted it; mid-scene = he's frustrated dad won't let him deeper in. Coherent
  as hates-the-labor / craves-the-inner-room — but the page doesn't yet mark the distinction.
- **Two attackers implied (nice if deliberate):** Tony beaten like a man beats a man; Vinny taken
  by a THING. Human muscle + magic operating together implies underworld structure in one ambush.
- **Wins:** Vin/Tone banter is lived-in fast; Tony's one-year quit = instant humanity right before
  he's destroyed (the everyone's-quitting-something fingerprint); "Devoid of sound and life"
  pre-plants the silent attack; empty container = clean misdirection; episode length + cliffhanger
  = correct serial form for the weekly habit.
- **Inheritance signature again (connective tissue):** forced legacy, dad's shadow, "you know how
  he started, kid." Vinny = inverse Chuck: Chuck fled the father's world and had to come home;
  Vinny is trapped inside it and wants deeper in.
- Mechanics: parked per standing rule (logged nothing; fast-draft density expected).

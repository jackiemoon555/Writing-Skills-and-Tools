## What a pack is
- **Seasonal Spotlight (Series 4 or 5):** 1 unowned base card **from the current or previous
  season** + 2 additional reward rolls.
- **Collector's Pack (Series 3, 4, or 5):** 1 unowned base card **from 3+ seasons ago** + 2 rolls.
- The card is RANDOM from that pool. The page does not describe any pick-your-card option.
- **Series complete:** if you own every base card in that series you can't buy its pack until new
  cards are added. If a BONUS roll hits a series you've completed you get tokens instead
  (Spotlight: S3 850 / S4 2,500 / S5 5,000; Collector's: S3 650 / S4 2,000 / S5 4,000).
- **Boosters** from bonus rolls go to the FIRST card the pack gave you, never to a bonus-roll card.
- **Bonus Card Protection:** a bonus card of a given type is guaranteed after X rolls where
  X × rate = 100% (their example: 2.67% S3 card in a Collector's S5 pack → guaranteed after 37 rolls).

Featured Set variants: variant price + the base card's series price if you don't own the base
(their example: a 5,000-token Ultimate variant of a Series 3 card = 6,000 if you lack the base).
Featured Set packs: every listed content has an equal rate; 1 card/variant + 2 bonus rolls.

## Headline read (Claude, 2026-09-24; "S3 incomplete" column, the likely one for a 123-card collection)
- **The guaranteed card is the whole product.** The two bonus rolls are ~40–45% boosters, ~23–24%
  tokens, ~23–24% credits, and a few % of extra cards/cosmetics.
- **Expected bonus value per pack (2 rolls), S3-incomplete column:**
  | Pack | Price | ≈ tokens back | ≈ credits back | any bonus CARD per pack |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Spotlight S5 | 6,000 | ~32 (0.5%) | ~59 | ~15% |
  | Collector's S5 | 6,000 | ~33 (0.6%) | ~60 | ~10% |
  | Spotlight S4 | 3,000 | ~28 (0.9%) | ~24 | ~7% |
  | Collector's S4 | 3,000 | ~28 (0.9%) | ~24 | ~5% |
  | Collector's S3 | 1,000 | ~27 (2.7%) | ~39 | ~3% |
- So: **a Series 3 card costs 1,000 tokens; a Series 5 card costs six of those.** Bonus rolls
  never move that math by more than a rounding error.
- **You cannot aim.** A pack gives a random unowned card from its pool. For a SPECIFIC card the
  pack is a lottery whose odds = 1 / (unowned cards in that pool) — the smaller your collection,
  the worse the aim. The protection guarantee only covers bonus-roll cards and needs 37–124+
  rolls — irrelevant at a few packs a month.
- Tokens are a real cap on the game for him; nothing here changes the earlier read that
  acquisition is the drag, and the packs make the drag random on top of expensive.

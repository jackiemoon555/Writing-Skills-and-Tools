## How the ladder works past 100 (sourced)

- **Rank 100 = Infinite, then a leaderboard.** Past 100 your rank is replaced by a leaderboard standing based on **Snap Points (SP)**, "based on your MMR and seasonal play"; lower number = better. (Official Help Center, "How do Ranks work?", fetched 10-01; page says it was last updated "237 days ago", about Feb 2026.) At launch of the system (Sept 2023) the write-up says winning cubes after Infinite gives SP (TouchArcade 2023-09-06). The exact cube-to-SP conversion is **not sourced**.
- **Cubes per rank: 7** ("you need 7 Cubes to go up a rank", Help Center). Older guides (outof.games, Screenrant 2023) say 10; the Help Center is newer and official, so I use 7. Rank 100 is a floor for the rest of the season (Help Center / MSZ FAQ).
- **Reset: sources disagree.** MSZ FAQ (page dated 2026-04-20): drop 30 ranks from current (not peak) to the nearest multiple of 10, then +3; Infinite resets to **73**. Help Center table: Infinite resets to **75**; ranks 90-99 reset to **73**; 80-89 to 65. His file's "about thirty" is right in spirit; the exact landing differs by source. By the MSZ formula 93 would land at 63; by the Help Center table, 73.
- **What "top 50" means: not sourced for the current game.** The in-game board is a ranking by SP; MSZ's Infinite Leaderboard page (a 2023-10 season page) says the official board shows Global / America / Asia / Europe / Others, with the metric not stated. I did not find the SP needed for rank 50, or a statement that "top 50" refers to global.
- **Season calendar: new season starts Oct 6.** MSZ schedule page: "Axis Inversion" runs through **October 6, 2026**; SnapComplete's data week also ends Oct 6 19:00 UTC; the MSZ datamine (unconfirmed) lists the October season as Zombie/Vampire themed with Zombie Giant-Man as the pass card, starting Oct 6. The MSZ page `october-2026-season/` returned **404**, so the official October name is not confirmed. An OTA balance update is listed for 10-01 but its contents were not published when I checked.

## What this does NOT tell him

- **The window is short.** SnapComplete's lists are "this week", a snap week that ends Oct 6 and is part-way through, from SnapComplete's synced players only. The 10-01 OTA may already have shifted these lists.
- **Top 50 is a points board, and the cutoff is unsourced.** Nothing I loaded gives the SP needed, the games played by leaders, or whether "top 50" is global or regional. A volume question (how many games) cannot be answered from these sources.
- **A deck list is not a result.** Win rates here are for the players who run each list, many of them strong; they say nothing about his own win rate or cube rate. Draft results (three 5-win runs) are a different mode.
- **Reset and cube rules are mixed-source.** The Help Center is ~237 days old and disagrees with the MSZ FAQ on the reset landing (75 vs 73); 2023 articles supply the per-game cube numbers.
- **Untapped's Infinite filter is behind its Premium paywall** (free window was ranks 10-79, a different population), so these lists come from one site.
